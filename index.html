About us

A quiet spa experience centered on comfort.

Before your visit.

Helpful answers to common questions so you can arrive relaxed and prepared.

Please arrive 5-10 minutes early so you have time to check in, settle down, and begin your session calmly.

If you have a recent injury, medical condition, or ongoing pain, please consult your healthcare provider first and let the spa know before your session begins.

Please call as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, so the appointment time can be made available to another guest.

Parking is typically available near the location. For the easiest visit, check nearby parking when you arrive at 1255 W Shaw Ave ste 100, Fresno, CA 93711.
